Selected and focused tickets now look different: focus is the outside ring, and the ticket open in the details panel gets a tint and an inside edge. Faded and outside-scope tickets keep readable text instead of fading it, and links outside a highlight stay faintly visible. Critical-path tickets say "Critical" on timeline rows, highlighted cards and in the details panel. Inputs, selects and menu buttons have edges you can see, and several colors were adjusted to meet contrast guidelines (the Story icon, links and the epic icon in dark mode, and "connected" in Settings). Windows High Contrast now marks the active view, pressed filters and the selected ticket.
