Field explanations no longer need a mouse hover. An ⓘ next to Sort by, Hide implied links, Whose tickets and the timeline's Unstarted from, Days / point and Unpointed opens the explanation on click, Enter or Space, and screen readers hear it with the field. When Domino can't tell who you are on a site, the ⓘ shows why. The Legends now say what each ticket-type letter means, and the Timeline Legend explains the over/under estimate badge.
